# DAY overnight destinations for aligned routes 125 and 136

Prepared October 10, 2026 local time from the **unpublished CRJ200 alignment** of released v1.2.4. **These are reviewed proposals only: no destination extensions, retimings or bank changes have been added to the working schedule or published.** The alignment remains the working baseline; the app remains on released v1.2.4.

## Recommended: route 125 to IND; route 136 to CAK

This is the strongest combined demand result in the nearby shortlist, with only isolated first-flight retimings and a one-minute shift of the DAY B1 window. Both markets have two current daily flights in each direction; each would rise to three. Neither currently has an early-morning departure to DAY. The existing IND–DAY departures are 14:47 and 19:00; CAK–DAY departures are 10:42 and 18:55.

DAY, IND, CAK and FWA times are **ET**; HOU arrival/departure times are marked **CT**. Flight numbers for the four additions are provisional replay numbers, not published assignments.

| 136 / AF-4 | DAY 21:35 → CAK 22:25 | — | Aircraft RONs in CAK |

The 21:35 departures fit existing DAY B9 and capture late inbound feed. IND and CAK provide the strongest connecting-demand scores of the nearby candidates. IND's evening demand is led by CLT, PIE and FLL traffic; its morning outbound demand emphasizes Texas, Florida and the Mid-Atlantic. CAK's evening feed emphasizes Florida, Texas, CGI and Great Lakes markets; its morning opportunity is especially Florida, BNA and Texas focused. Local DAY O-D alone is tiny, so these are connection-led additions.

## Bank and connection details

DAY B1 shifts from **05:00–06:00 to 05:01–06:01**, retaining its 60-minute duration and all currently valid assigned touches. The earliest existing valid B1 touch is the 05:09 DAY–JAX departure, so no flight is displaced by the start shift. The standing 04:30 DAY–BHM bank exception remains unchanged. The new 06:00 FWA departure is inside the shifted window; no new bank exception is needed.

Both new feeders connect to the later B1 departures:

|---|---|---:|---:|

| HOU | 05:53 | 40 min | 33 min |

Later banks provide additional onward choices. The new arrivals do not feed the already-departed 04:30 BHM or 05:09 JAX flights. The modeled flight-connection records include 18 onward flights for IND and 14 for CAK after applying O-D/circuity filters; the clock-only 30–240-minute screen identifies 19 direct DAY departures after each arrival. These counts describe different filters and are not summed.

The two aircraft have exactly the minimum 40-minute turn into their existing originators. CAK's ATW connection is also exactly the 30-minute passenger floor; these timings offer limited delay margin. Aircraft feasibility has been validated; the six-hour destination stays are not a claim that a single crew can operate both the late arrival and 04:30 departure without a separate crew/rest plan.

## Demand comparison and alternatives

Opportunity units below are from the pinned BTS O-D model, allocated among all zero-, one- and two-stop alternatives. **They are seat-uncapped full-capture relative-choice comparisons, not passenger forecasts, achievable loads, incremental travelers or profit.** They rank connectivity and do not establish that a 50-seat aircraft could carry every modeled unit. Local O-D allocations are about 0.1 units on IND/CAK additions; almost all modeled opportunity is connecting.

|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| CAK | 2 | +10 min | 432.0 / 289.2 | Strongest combined opportunity; +1-minute bank-window shift needed |
| IND | 2 | +3 min | 389.8 / 258.4 | Second strongest combined opportunity; best recommended partner |
| CMH | 2 | +0 min | 327.5 / 283.0 | Strong alternative; zero delay if assigned to 125 |

**Less timing disruption:** 125→CMH / 126 CMH→DAY 04:30–05:08 keeps DAY–HOU at 05:50. Pair it with CAK on 136 for a ten-minute FWA delay and the one-minute B1-window shift, or IND on 136 for only a three-minute FWA delay with the bank unchanged. The CMH/IND portfolio has no slower fastest market in the audit, but less combined connecting opportunity than IND/CAK.

**Second-frequency priority:** 125→LEX 21:35–22:21 / 126 LEX→DAY 04:30–05:16 requires DAY–HOU 05:56–07:34 CT arrival, a six-minute delay; its 09:50 CT HOU return stays fixed. LEX grows from one to two flights each way. It passes jointly with either IND or CAK on 136, but its morning score is only about 33.4 units. It is a defensible service-coverage option, rather than the strongest demand option.

FWA and TOL already depart for DAY at 04:30; another return then fails the 30-minute spacing floor. SDF and CRW have no evening timing in the current DAY banks satisfying §2.6 when paired with the 04:30 return. SBN needs +11 minutes, CRW +12, PIT +17 and GRR +18; they were excluded from the recommended modest-change shortlist (maximum +10). PIT is a worthwhile later sensitivity because it has just one current flight each way, but it requires a larger first-flight and bank-window shift. No claim of physical impossibility is made for these larger-retiming alternatives.

## Full joint verification

All seven shortlisted portfolios passed structural, operating, overnight-turn, planning and fixed gate/stand checks. The recommended IND/CAK portfolio has **zero effective operating errors**, 172 inherited advisories, and no new operating exceptions. Stand utilization remains **16 claims / 3,717 minutes**. The 11-day AE and AF cycles remain closed and retain independent maintenance RONs: AE's maximum non-MX run is 10, AF's is 7, with no grace required. §2.6 passes for the additions and retimings.

Only original flights **1503 (+3 minutes)** and **1873 (+10 minutes)** change their clocks. Every other original flight keeps its fleet, airports, route/line/day, and departure/arrival clocks; sequence indexes shift where the early feeder is inserted. Exactly four flights are added in each reviewed portfolio, yielding 1,174 flights in its reconstructed candidate. No candidate changes aircraft-days or fleet inventory.

The recommended joint network audit finds **21 directional markets with faster best itineraries**, no market losing all valid connections, and no pre-existing connecting-choice tuple lost from either retimed flight. One fastest itinerary, **TOL→HOU**, becomes three minutes slower; its pinned O-D value is zero. This remains a reported schedule tradeoff, not a claim that no real traveler could be affected. Later-flight clocks do not cascade. The 52 latest-release regression checks pass against released v1.2.4; superseded releases were not tested.
