## What B1 already provides

MCI B1 is currently 04:45–05:45, with one arrival (IND at 04:55) and 11 departures. Ten departures are reachable by clock, but the O-D/circuity model supports IND feed to only nine of them. AUS and RDU currently have zero modeled connecting allocation. The other flights already receive some IND feed; JAX also carries onward connections after reaching JAX. The improvement is more geographic feed and useful connecting demand, rather than creating the first connection to every flight.

## Validation and tradeoffs

The recommended package passes structural, operating hard-stop, curfew, minimum-turn, overnight-turn, §2.6 spacing, planning and fixed gate/stand checks: **zero effective operating errors**, no new exceptions and no newly introduced advisory IDs. There are 170 advisories versus 172 in the base. Stand use stays at 16 claims / 3,717 minutes. The existing unrelated MAX9 line A length advisory remains; the proposal does not rebuild that line.

The full network audit finds **79 directional markets with faster best itineraries**, and **no market loses all valid connections**. None of the ten retimed flights loses an existing connecting-choice tuple. Seven fastest itineraries become slower: IND–SFB by ten minutes, and IND–OMA/ELP/ICT/FSD/SHV/CID by fifteen. A slower best itinerary and the loss of an available choice are different measures; both were checked.

SGF arrives only 30 minutes before the 05:50 wave, exactly the passenger-connection floor; early SGF delays can break that feed. Destination overnight intervals are about 4½–4¾ hours. These are aircraft feasibility checks, not a crew duty/rest plan; separate crews or an appropriate crew rotation must support the late arrivals and 04:30 originators.

## Alternatives considered

- **Smaller change:** SDF/SBN/GRR without SGF passes with a one-minute bank shift and only four original retimings (+10 SHV, +8 CID, +10 SFB, +1 AUS). It improves 58 best itineraries but leaves RDU without geographically useful feed. The saved overlay is `schedule_7_v1_2_5_mci_sdf112_sbn135_grr715.json`. Its tighter minimum turns and connection margins are recorded in the review.
- **DSM instead of SGF:** the screened three-destination DSM package has more modeled demand, but gives DSM a fourth frequency instead of SGF a second. It needs seven minutes of bank shift and sixteen-minute departure delays. SGF is preferred for service coverage and inbound geography. The comparison is not a full four-destination DSM+GRR validation.
- **AI maintenance realignment:** moving flight 1733 MCI–DSM from route 153’s 19:55 terminator to route 154’s 05:35 departure creates a replacement MCI maintenance night and preserves the 07:25 DSM return. It passes physically, but worsens numerous DSM best itineraries and removes OKC–DSM’s only valid modeled connection. That tradeoff is too large for the additional SBN flying.
- **Fully connecting AUS:** moving AUS to 05:50 and adjusting its return, the other AUS return and the next SFB departure passes operating checks, but five markets lose all connections: AUS–ICT, AUS–TUL and FAR/DLH/PIA–SFB (61.1 underlying O-D units). It is rejected. An earlier AUS departure can use IND, but still misses TUL; fixing TUL’s turn would cascade into SYR bank timing.
- **Route 507:** SBN/SDF feeder returns need +13/+15 minutes on PGD, shrinking the immediate PGD turn to 27/25 minutes. Delaying the PGD return pushes the subsequent MKE departure beyond its bank. Cleaner aircraft fits exist.
- **Route 509:** its 22:40 MCI arrival leaves no existing bank departure after a 40-minute turn; useful eastern destinations also arrive too late.
- **Route 336:** nearby Central-time destinations return around 05:18–05:30 and require a larger delay to its 05:35 ELP originator; eastern destinations would terminate after 00:01 from its late MCI arrival.
- **CMH, FWA and other markets:** CMH has strong demand but requires a broader morning retiming; FWA passes with a five-minute bank shift but has only about 33.4/17.6 modeled evening/morning units. New markets were screened as well as existing markets. The full screen records feasible nearby options and rejects.
