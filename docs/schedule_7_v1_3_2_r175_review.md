# R175 holistic review

Reviewed October 14, 2026 against the cumulative v1.3.2 draft, including the accepted R501 RFD replacement. **R175 has not been modified.** The recommended BWI option is a proposal for review; publication remains unauthorized.

## Recommendation

Add a CRJ200 PWM–BWI round trip during the 5h17 PWM hold. Advance the ISP originator eight minutes and the first SYR–PWM flight ten minutes. Preserve every original flight, all afternoon/evening clocks, both endpoints, the AN maintenance rotation and every other route.

|---|---|---|
| F1892 ISP–SYR | 06:55–07:53 | **06:47–07:45** |
| F1699 SYR–PWM | 08:35–09:43 | **08:25–09:33** |
| New PWM–BWI | — | **10:13–11:43** |
| New BWI–PWM | — | **12:23–13:53** |
| F1704 PWM–SYR | 15:00–16:08 | Unchanged |
| F1907 SYR–ACY | 16:50–17:54 | Unchanged |
| F1866 ACY–PHF | 19:20–20:14 | Unchanged |
| F1746 PHF–AVP | 20:55–22:03 | Unchanged |

R175 grows from six to eight flights and **6h20 to 9h20 block flying**, without another aircraft. The PWM hold becomes **40 minutes before BWI and 67 minutes afterward**, with 40 at BWI. The other turns are 40 at SYR, 42 at SYR, 86 at ACY and 41 at PHF. The longest same-day hold anywhere on R175 becomes **1h26**, at ACY. The original AVP terminator and next-day R176 05:05 AVP originator remain unchanged.

The two small advances are necessary for the tested BWI gate fit. At the unchanged morning clocks, the earliest PWM departure is 10:23 and BWI arrival 11:53; a 40-minute BWI turn lasts through 12:33 and fails the fixed two-gate inventory. Advancing SYR–PWM to 08:25 makes PWM 09:33/10:13 and BWI 11:43/12:23 feasible. ISP–SYR must arrive at 07:45 to leave 40 minutes before 08:25; hence its 06:47 departure. Both SYR touches remain inside existing SYR B2. No other flight, bank, inventory or policy changes are required.

## Demand and network effects

PWM–BWI is PWM's **third-largest local O-D market** in the pinned directional matrix, after SFB and RFD: **32.4 PWM→BWI and 32.6 BWI→PWM passengers/day**. Those larger markets require 192 and 159 minutes each way on the pinned CRJ200 block profile, respectively, and cannot fit this hold as a simple insertion. BWI requires 90 each way. There is currently no PWM–BWI nonstop. These are underlying market totals, not predicted flight loads; the source already uses a dampened own/primary blend for secondary airports.

| Journey | Current fastest | Proposed fastest |
|---|---:|---:|
| PWM→BWI | 3h14, one connection | **1h30 nonstop** |
| BWI→PWM | 2h48, one connection | **1h30 nonstop** |
| SFB→PWM | 4h16 | **4h08** |
| PIE→PWM | 4h31 | **4h23** |
| PGD→PWM | 4h57 | **4h47** |
| BNA→PWM | 3h53 | **3h43** |
| SRQ→PWM | 4h48 | **4h38** |

The geographic app audit preserves **every existing itinerary choice and all direct/one-stop coverage**. BWI becomes nonstop in both directions; there is no claim that previously unserved one-stop markets are opened. **Fifteen fastest journeys improve. Twenty-two ISP-origin fastest journeys increase by exactly eight minutes**, because the earlier originator feeds the same retained onward departures. ISP→SRQ changes 4h36→4h44 and ISP→JAX 3h58→4h06; no connection disappears. Fourteen slower markets have nonzero O-D and eight have zero in the pinned matrix.

The insertion's local-only opportunity indices are 32.4 / 32.6; no hub-feed credit is invented for BWI. The existing R175 ISP–SYR and SYR–PWM flights have materially greater connecting value than their narrow local demand, so changing their timing wholesale requires a network comparison, not just more aircraft block.

## Why keep the start close to its current time?

The 06:55 ISP departure supplies SYR B2 and is not simply idle time waiting to be filled. Moving it to 04:30 or 05:10 without useful added flying loses **ten existing itinerary choices** and slows 21 or 22 fastest journeys. It adds no flights and leaves the PWM hold unchanged. An eight-minute advance alone preserves choices but lengthens 22 elapsed journeys and adds no flying; it is worthwhile here only as part of the BWI insertion.

An extra closed round trip from ISP before the current 06:55 originator cannot fit. Even an ISP–SYR or ISP–BWI turn needs 196 minutes including two 40-minute turns, while the earliest legal 04:30 departure supplies only 145 minutes to the current originator. A closed SYR turn after an earlier ISP arrival also encounters bank limits: the earliest possible SYR–ROC–SYR sequence returns at 08:06 and cannot turn into SYR–PWM until 08:46, after B2's 08:45 end. Shortening blocks or waiving a bank was not assumed.

The remainder of R175 is already purposeful hub flying. Its SYR 16:08→16:50 and PHF 20:14→20:55 turns are 42 and 41 minutes. ACY has 86 minutes, which cannot accommodate another out-and-back with 40-minute turns. The 22:03 AVP terminator supports an existing destination RON; adding a late AVP departure would violate the destination departure window. These are not additional useful holds to fill independently.

## Alternatives screened

Thirteen timetable variants were checked; ten pass structural, operating, planning, next-day turn and physical gates/stands. The recommended BWI option has **zero effective operating errors and zero hard stops**, unchanged fleet use, and unchanged stand use at **15 claims / 3,061 minutes**. The inherited AM maintenance grace remains reported. The existing runway-data check remains unavailable.

| Alternative | Finding |
|---|---|
| BWI at unchanged morning clocks, three departures tested | All fail BWI's fixed two-gate inventory. |
| Recommended BWI with the two small advances | Passes all checks; no existing choice lost; 15 faster / 22 slower by eight minutes. |
| Extra PWM–SYR turn | Passes with four retimings, including three-minute delays to PWM–SYR and SYR–ACY. Adds 2h16 block, but connecting indices are only 33.1 / 14.2 and 39 fastest journeys slow. |
| PWM–PIT turn | Passes with four retimings. Only 9.6 / 9.3 daily local O-D and 39 slower fastest journeys; weaker than BWI. |
| 04:30 ISP start, 06:08 SYR–PWM, then PHF turn; three timings | Pass physically and add 3h28 block, but lose 21 itinerary choices and slow 25 fastest journeys. Two one-stop gains, PNS→PWM and DAY→PWM, have only 3.5 / 1.7 underlying daily demand. |
| Same very early start, then BWI | Passes physically but loses 21 choices and one-stop coverage in two markets. The smaller BWI repair avoids those losses. |

Representative harmful PHF tradeoffs include CAK→PWM rising from 3h03 to 5h13 and ISP→MCI from 4h22 to 6h47. Legality and increased utilization alone do not justify that option.
