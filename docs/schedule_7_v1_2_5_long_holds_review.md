ALB–PHF is the clearest addition: it supplies a second nonstop, diversifies ALB's predominantly SYR-based connections, and supports both directions. SWF–PHF gives SWF access to Florida and coastal demand through a new hub. Its pinned local PHF O-D is zero; the recommendation is entirely about connections. SWF–DAY would add a second nonstop but scores only about **2.6 outbound / 0.7 return** in the standalone review, so it is substantially weaker.

The two SYR round trips position aircraft outward for strong **B3 inbound feeders**. The BTV return complements the 07:00, 15:15 and 18:00 BTV departures with a 09:48 option. HPN is selected over ISP for its missing morning SYR service; ISP's slightly higher joint return score, 108.9, mainly adds a later alternative to an existing 06:55 originator. **The weak outbound directions are material.** BTV/HPN are lower-priority connecting proposals, not balanced round trips. Final economics should account for operating the positioning legs and realistic demand capture/capacity on the returns. If additions must attract useful demand in both directions, retain these two holds and pursue only the PHF turns for now.

## Holistic alternatives tested

- Returning into SYR B3 without changing original clocks works for route 318; route 161 misses the required turn by two minutes. The small DAY adjustment above fixes the latter without losing existing connecting-choice tuples.
- Later BTV/HPN/ISP departures alone encounter gates or §2.6 spacing conflicts. The tested 08:25 BTV turn with a nine-minute SRQ delay also fails in the combined four-route package. A new AVP morning pairing was screened, but the tested later joint case fails physical gate validation.
- A validated alternative departs SYR for BTV at **08:20**, returns **10:44**, and moves its SRQ departure to **11:24**. To accept Florida feed, it advances SRQ–SYR by one minute, FLL–SYR and PIE–SYR by four, and SFB–SYR by five, bringing all four to SYR at 07:50. BTV's outbound score rises to 14.6, but its return falls to 116.7; total opportunity for the HPN joint package rises only from about **502.6 to 510.1**. Several already-served itineraries become 1–5 minutes longer because their originators leave earlier. That modest gain does not justify making the broader changes the default recommendation.
- The timing search is bounded: broad 15-minute screens, explicit boundary and fine-timing cases, and full validation/demand comparison of finalists. It is not a proof of a global mathematical optimum.
