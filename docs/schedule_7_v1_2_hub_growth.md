# One-spare-MAX9 JAX and DAY market review

> Release update, October 1, 2026: the approved cumulative changes are now
> published as v1.2.0. See `schedule_7_v1_2_0_release.md` and
> `regression_policy.md` for current release and testing policy. The
> feasibility/default statements below describe the original draft stage.


The strongest JAX candidates with 1–3 daily nonstops are SFB, FLL, PGD,
PIE and SRQ. The recommended first addition is one round trip each on
JAX–SFB, JAX–FLL and JAX–PIE. PGD fits the available aircraft time but its
proposed flights have substantially weaker demand opportunity. At DAY,
RFD leads, followed by CMH, CAK, IND and PIT.

## Scope and sources

This review starts from released v1.1.6 and the approved v1.2 CRJ900 and
MAX9 line overlays, `schedule_7_v1_2_0_feasibility_02`. It screens all
fleets, not only CRJ700/900 service. The directional demand pin remains
`bts-db1c-6mo-jul2025-apr2026-v7`.

The user authorized one additional active MAX9 from existing inventory.
Each option increases active MAX9 aircraft-days from 21 to 22 out of 35,
leaving 13 spare. Existing flights, numbers, IDs, times, sequences, fleets,
pairings and bank assignments are preserved. Only route/day labels are
normalized to insert the new day into MAX9 Line A. A becomes 12 days and
B stays 10. Physical inventories, operating policy and bank windows are
unchanged. These are mutually exclusive unpublished proposals; the approved
overlay chain and published v1.1.6 default remain unchanged.

All times here are Eastern. Precise allocation values are generated by the
analysis script and included in the workbook. Each market increases from
one to two daily nonstops each way. The new day has 346 block minutes,
six legs and a 254-minute JAX day hold. It supplies a faster best eligible

optimum or revenue-maximizing solution.

claims totaling 693 minutes. Systemwide use becomes 22 claims / 6,245
minutes, versus 20 / 5,552 in the approved draft. All passenger handling
remains on gates; no extra JAX stand use is required. The new MAX9 leaves
JAX before its 18:49–19:25 period with all 16 gates occupied.

## Optional PGD extension

`config/proposals/schedule_7_v1_2_0_jax_pgd.json` adds JAX–PGD
11:25–12:27 and PGD–JAX 13:13–14:15 during the JAX hold. The route then
has eight legs and 470 block minutes. It also preserves every existing
flight and passes ground checks. PGD becomes two daily flights each way.

The new PGD flights receive only about 136 and 118 total opportunity units
after competition with existing flights. SRQ in a similar slot was weaker,
about 87 outbound and 56 inbound. PGD's high current-market ranking does
not guarantee that an extra midday MAX9 would fill consistently. Keep this
extension conditional on feeder capacity, attainable capture and operating
economics rather than adding it simply to occupy the aircraft.

## Model and remaining commercial questions

All nonstops compete with all qualifying one-stop options through BHM,
DAY, JAX, MCI, PHF and SYR. Connections require 30–240 minutes and circuity
at most 2.25. Nonstop weights are `1 / block²`; one-stop weights are
`0.35 / elapsed² / circuity²`. Each directional O-D demand pool is allocated
proportionately among its eligible choices, reconciling to that pool.
Inbound feed supports a hub departure; outbound feed supports a hub
arrival. Hub-to-hub flights can have both from distinct O-D markets.

Each proposal is reallocated jointly with the existing network. It does
not count frequency additions as new demand. Leg totals must not be summed
as unique travelers when the same itinerary uses two new legs. The report's
`uniqueAllocatedItineraryOpportunity` counts an itinerary once.

The proxy assumes full CAA capture and has no seat constraints, so large
values can greatly exceed the airplane's seating. Confirm actual aircraft
configuration, feeder availability/spill, achievable capture, yield and
trip cost before selecting capacity. Airport catchment overlap, seasonality,
surface-travel substitution on short stages, crew duties and connection
reliability are also material. Pinned airport data lacks runway and payload
inputs; those checks remain unevaluated rather than being declared passed.

## Reproduction and validation

Run `python scripts/analyze_schedule_7_v1_2_hub_growth.py` to regenerate the
market screen, three proposal overlays, and joint demand comparisons.
Build an option with, for example:

```bash
python scripts/build_schedule_7_v1_2_feasibility.py \
  --extra-overlay config/proposals/schedule_7_v1_2_0_jax_core.json
```

The ordinary runner still builds only the two approved overlays. All three
options pass structural and planning validation, have zero effective
operating errors / hard-stop failures, and pass overnight continuity.
They retain existing approved overrides without adding any. Nonblocking
warnings remain. `tests/test_v120_hub_growth.py` verifies additive scope,
unchanged policies and physical inventory, one extra aircraft-day, closed
compliant lines, the validators, and the unchanged published default.
